# Google Search and Meta: small, measurable launch

Prepared September 24, 2026. **Planning only: no accounts changed, campaigns created, money spent, or messages sent.** Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Search Console, billing and existing conversion configuration have not been inspected in an authenticated account. Confirm them before adding anything.

The objective is **qualified repair inquiries received by Tony, followed by profitable booked work**. Google Search is the first test because it can reach someone actively looking for repair help. Meta is the second test for genuine repair and specialty-tool evidence. This is a channel hypothesis, not a Fort Myers cost-per-lead forecast.

## Current constraint

The website's conventional form prepares an editable text to `(239) 397-2048`; the customer must send it. The website cannot confirm receipt, store a lead, or prove Tony was notified. Bay One is off. Beside exists but its account, routing and integrations are unverified. Shopmonkey is obsolete.

Do not optimize either platform toward a fake `lead` event fired on a call tap, form start, or text-draft opening. A received customer text or answered repair inquiry can be counted in Tony's private log. This can support a small manually measured pilot after the delivery test below; it does not complete the original durable website-intake requirement. Website conversion optimization waits for an approved, tested receiver. Native Meta form submissions are a separate proposed channel and need their own storage, access and follow-up test.

## Google Search pilot

Use one Search campaign with two or three tightly related ad groups. Start with exact and phrase service-intent keywords; review actual search terms because matching can extend beyond literal wording. Keep Search partners and broader network expansion off for the initial comparison. Use responsive search ads with a call asset; Google removed new call-only ad creation in February 2026. [Google's migration notice](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/9129922?hl=en)

| Ad group | Draft intent examples, not verified search volumes | Relevant existing destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No-start / stranded | `[mobile mechanic fort myers]`, `"car won't start mechanic"`, `"no start diagnosis near me"` | `/no-start-diagnosis-fort-myers`; link the no-start guide as supporting education |
| Electrical diagnosis | `"auto electrical repair fort myers"`, `"battery keeps dying mechanic"` | `/auto-electrical-repair-fort-myers`; related battery-drain guide |
| A/C diagnosis | `"car ac repair fort myers"`, `"car ac warm at idle repair"` | `/ac-repair-fort-myers`; related idle A/C guide |

Urgent symptom pages can earn organic visits from broader questions. Paid keywords should initially include repair/mechanic intent so the budget does not primarily buy nationwide DIY research. Add cooling, diesel or programming campaigns later only when Tony confirms the exact job, tooling, service area and profitable capacity; a frightening symptom does not prove a high-value repair.

**Local controls:** target the owner-approved cities/ZIPs, initially narrower than the tentative 75–100-mile maximum travel range. Select Google's **Presence: people in or regularly in** included locations. Exclude unserviceable areas and verify the customer's city before promising dispatch. Location targeting is an estimate, so review geographic results and out-of-area inquiries. [Google location targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453995?hl=en)

**Starter negatives:** mechanic jobs, mechanic salary, mechanic school, training, repair manual, wiring diagram PDF, tool rental, parts wholesale. Add towing-only, bodywork, motorcycles or other exclusions only after Tony says those services are not offered. Avoid broad negatives such as `cost`, `price`, `battery`, `diagnosis`, or `dealer`; they can remove valuable prospects. Check singular/plural variants and actual search terms; negative keywords do not cover close variants. [Google negative keywords](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en-GB)

**Draft responsive ad assets** (headlines under 30 characters; descriptions under 90):

- Headlines: `Mobile Mechanic Fort Myers` / `Car Won't Start? Call Tony` / `Discuss Your Repair Timeline`
- Descriptions: `Tell Tony the symptoms and location. He confirms availability and next steps.`
- Alternative: `Need your vehicle back? Ask what timing is realistic for your repair.`
- Supporting sitelinks: No-Start Help, Electrical Diagnosis, A/C Repair, Repair Guides.

Do not imply dealership indifference, guaranteed same-day completion, immediate dispatch, rental coverage, or exclusive 24/7 availability. Owner-confirmed 24/7 contact availability can be used with the same visible dispatch qualification as the site. Paid overnight coverage should be enabled only for periods Tony can actually handle; display hours alone do not prove missed-call recovery.

**Bidding:** for a small pilot without reliable imported outcomes, use a controlled click bid strategy with a ceiling derived from the allowable lead cost and the account's actual keyword estimates. Treat it as a temporary traffic-buying method, evaluated by received inquiries. Move to conversion-based bidding only after verified outcomes are supplied. Do not invent a target CPA from untested clicks or install a second tag over an existing integration.

**Calls:** propose Google call reporting as part of the approval card. It uses a Google forwarding number and can report connected calls and duration. A duration-qualified call is still only a proxy for a qualified repair inquiry. Keep Tony's permanent public number on the site unless a separately reviewed website-call measurement change is approved. Call recording is outside this plan. [Google call reporting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454052?hl=en), [call outcome measurement](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6197479?hl=en)

## Meta pilot: Facebook and Instagram together

Propose one **Leads** campaign with one local ad set and two genuine creative variants, using an Instant Form so the platform records a submission even while the website receiver is inactive. Start with lead submissions; conversion-lead optimization is a later option requiring supported CRM/Conversions API outcome feedback. Meta's official lead-generation guide describes these distinctions and form options. Exact current account availability must be checked before creation. [Meta lead-generation guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf)

1. Set the approved local geography as an actual audience control, not merely a suggested interest. Inspect expansion and placement settings in the account. Start with adult local prospects and minimal interest layering; judge leads by their actual city and repair need.
2. Variant A: a real short tool-in-use clip, captioned with what it measures. Copy: **“The right test helps narrow the next step. Tell Tony what your vehicle is doing.”** Name a tool or capability only after its evidence is checked.
3. Variant B: Tony or a permission-cleared repair photo. Copy: **“Before you rearrange your week, talk to Tony. Explain the problem and ask what repair timing is realistic.”** No same-day guarantee.
4. Keep the form short: name, mobile number, city/ZIP and main problem. Vehicle and starts/drivable details can be optional or collected in Tony's reply. Use a review step if available; test whether it improves qualified inquiries rather than assuming more friction is better.
5. Include an accurate privacy link and clear repair-only contact permission: **“I ask Perfect Timing Auto Repair to contact me by call or text about this repair inquiry.”** No preselected marketing permission. The confirmation screen must not say Tony was notified unless that was verified.
6. Test access, notification, retrieval and deletion with synthetic data. Tony needs an authenticated recovery inbox plus a named person checking it on the agreed schedule. Do not assume Beside receives Meta leads. If the routing cannot be proven, keep Meta paused.

Meta's current public product/help and Lead Ads Terms pages redirected to login during this research. Recheck [Lead Ads Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/leadgen/tos) and the account's privacy/permission requirements before implementation; this plan does not establish compliance or account eligibility. The existing site privacy notice will need to identify Meta and any actually selected retrieval provider before this channel is activated. Do not upload customer lists or enable remarketing as a default; Meta requires appropriate rights and a lawful basis for customer-list audience use. [Meta customer-list terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/customaudience/update)

## Proposed budget and decision rule

Illustrative first authorization request: **$300 Google Search over 14 days**, followed by **$100 Meta over 14 days** after its separate delivery gate. Reserve 75% for active search demand and 25% for the creative experiment. These are media allocations, not approved spending, promised lead counts or statistically conclusive tests. Production/management time and any approved integration fees are separate.

Use a Google campaign total budget with start/end dates where supported and verify its readback. Current Google documentation supports total budgets for Search; average daily budgets behave differently and can spend up to twice the daily average on a day. A `$20/day` setting is not a `$20` daily cap. For Meta, read back the offered lifetime/campaign/account spending limit and end date before approval; do not claim a daily average is a hard cap. [Google total-budget support](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/16924635?hl=en), [Google spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637/about-spending-limits)

Set economics with Tony before launch:

`allowable cost per received inquiry = conservative booking rate × gross contribution per completed job × chosen advertising share`

Illustration only: 25% booking × $400 contribution after parts, variable labor and travel × 30% advertising share = **$30 per received inquiry**, or **$120 per booked job**. At that assumption, $300 needs 10 received inquiries and approximately 2–3 booked jobs; these are decision thresholds, not predictions. A higher ticket with expensive parts or long travel can still produce a worse result.

Pause immediately for broken routing, lost access, misleading creative or inability to respond. At the agreed checkpoint, investigate an ad group that has spent twice its allowable inquiry cost without a qualified inquiry. Allow the real booking/completion lag before judging job economics. Increase spend only after enough completed outcomes support positive contribution, Tony has capacity, and the owner approves the new ceiling. If volume is too small, report uncertainty rather than declaring a winner.

## Measurement that reflects what happened

Keep customer details in Tony's approved private operational inbox. Maintain a separate access-controlled outcome log with a random inquiry ID, received date, source/campaign code, in-area yes/no, supported job yes/no, contacted/booked/closed outcome and contribution. Deduplicate repeat contacts about the same repair; platform totals are not automatically distinct customers. Do not put this log or customer records in the public repository.

| Signal | What can be claimed | How it informs decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tap to call / text draft | Intent to contact only | Secondary usability measurement |
| Received text / connected call | Contact occurred; not automatically qualified | Tony records source where known; unknown stays unknown |
| Saved Meta form | Platform received the form | Reconcile with retrieval and follow-up; submission is not Tony notification |
| Qualified inquiry | Supported repair, serviceable area, usable callback, real prospect | Compare cost per qualified inquiry |
| Booked / completed job | Actual operational outcome | Compare acquisition cost and gross contribution |

Use fixed non-personal campaign labels such as `google/search/no_start_v1` and `meta/paid_social/tools_v1`. Never send names, mobile numbers, symptom text or form values into analytics, URL parameters or ad event labels. Current site metrics are disabled; adding UTMs alone does not create a working measurement system. Do not automatically scrape SMS bodies, upload contact lists, or turn on enhanced conversions. Any future offline import must use its approved dedicated integration and permission process, not general analytics event fields. [Google Analytics PII guidance](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6366371?hl=en)

Daily pilot review: spend, received inquiries, missed/unanswered contacts, in-area proportion and booked outcomes. Weekly: completed contribution, search terms, creative comparison and service capacity. Preserve a dated settings export before each approved change. Rollback is to pause the affected campaigns, verify they are paused, stop any scheduled restart, and leave the existing website contact path intact.
