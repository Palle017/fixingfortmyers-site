# Perfect Timing: “Your day needs a plan.”

Planning proposal · September 24, 2026 · No spending approved or campaign launched.

## Concept

People need their vehicles for work, school runs and everyday life. Lead with a practical conversation: can this repair realistically fit today, and what should the customer plan for? Perfect Timing assesses the job and communicates expected timing; Tony confirms scope, parts and availability before making commitments. This addresses the frustration of uncertain repair schedules without criticizing dealerships or claiming faster repairs.

Use an authentic existing photo of Tony with a vehicle, subject to usage approval. Suggested overlay: **“Need your car for tomorrow? Let’s talk through the repair.”** Do not depict a promised turnaround, stocked parts, courtesy vehicle or unverified specialty equipment. Any specialty-tools proof should come from the separately reviewed capability proposal.

## Exactly two prioritized platforms

1. **Google Search — first priority for qualified repair demand.** Target a Tony-approved service area and tightly relevant repair-intent searches; begin with phrase/exact themes such as “mobile mechanic Fort Myers” and “auto repair Fort Myers.” Only advertise specific repair categories Tony confirms. Review actual searches and exclude irrelevant jobs, training, DIY and parts-only shopping. Google documents that Search reaches people actively searching for services, which supports this priority; it does not establish a local CPL. Start with calls and the matching page during periods Tony can reliably handle inquiries. [Google Search campaign documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9510373?hl=en).

2. **Meta Facebook/Instagram — second priority for the story and a measured lead test.** Use one local audience and the relatable schedule-focused creative above. Test the Leads objective with a short instant form collecting contact details, vehicle, location, symptoms and desired timing, only after notification/retrieval is verified. Meta’s own guide documents collecting interest with instant forms; a form submission still needs reachability and service qualification. This recommendation is an inference about fit: Meta can introduce the message while people browse, whereas Search starts with expressed repair intent. Avoid optimizing for cheap traffic or text-draft opens. [Meta lead-generation guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf).

Google Local Services is not a third recommendation or an assumed route to launch. Google requires a public, verified Business Profile; current project evidence says verification is pending. Category/location eligibility and any additional screening have not been checked. [Google requirement](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/13538718?hl=en-GB).

## Benchmarks and illustrative test economics

Verified publisher figures: WordStream/LocaliQ’s 2026 **Google + Microsoft Search** automotive repair/service/parts CPL is **$29.96**. It is a broad category benchmark, not Google-only or a Fort Myers forecast. [2026 search report](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks).

Facebook’s 2025 all-industry Leads CPL is **$27.66**; the newer 2026 report states **$27.39**, but supplies no automotive repair/service/parts Leads category. Auto-for-sale leads and repair traffic CPC are different measures. Its methodology also lists a leads sample ending June 30, 2028, a future date as of this proposal; treat that inconsistency as unresolved. These Meta figures are not directly comparable to the search repair benchmark or our qualified-lead definition. [2025 report](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025), [2026 report](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2026).

**Illustration only: $600 media cap over 2–3 weeks**, $450 Search / $150 Meta, released only after explicit budget approval and launch gates. Run Search first if tracking or response capacity is limited. This small sample can expose bad targeting and missed leads; it cannot establish a reliable winning platform. Fees, creative work and any tracking costs are excluded and require separate agreement. Platform budget controls must be checked before launch; a daily budget alone is not proof of a hard test cap.

The following are deliberately uncertain planning assumptions, not quoted market forecasts. Actual costs can fall outside them, including no qualified leads or bookings.

| Platform | Cost per received inquiry assumption | Qualification assumption | Implied cost per qualified lead | Booking rate among qualified | Implied cost per booked job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Search | $30–$60 | 60–80% | $38–$100 | 40–60% | $63–$250 |
| Meta | $20–$50 | 30–60% | $33–$167 | 30–50% | $67–$556 |

A **qualified lead** is a genuine, reachable local customer with a vehicle/service Tony can consider. A click, opened SMS draft, spam, duplicate or unreachable form entry is not qualified. A received inquiry requires evidence in the receiving phone/inbox or captured Meta form; a booked job requires Tony and customer to agree on the job/appointment.

Formulas: **qualified CPL = spend ÷ unique qualified leads**; **cost per booked job = spend ÷ confirmed booked jobs = inquiry CPL ÷ qualification rate ÷ booking rate**. With zero bookings, report “no bookings” and spend, not a zero cost.

Sensitivity example: at $40/inquiry, 70% qualification and 50% booking, Search costs about $114/booked job. At $25/inquiry, 40% qualification and 40% booking, Meta costs about $156/booked job despite its cheaper initial inquiry. Halving either qualification or booking doubles acquisition cost. Track completed jobs/cancellations and contribution after labor, parts and travel before judging profitability. Tony must set an acceptable acquisition ceiling from actual margins.

## Minimum setup before paid launch

- **Prove reception and recovery.** Test a real phone’s call and customer-sent SMS to Tony, verify the receiving record and a reply, and verify missed-call visibility and manual follow-up. No test messages were sent for this proposal. Current site only prepares native SMS drafts; it provides neither durable lead storage nor delivery receipts. Draft abandonment cannot be recovered unless contact details actually reach an inbox or separately approved durable capture.
- **Use the existing Beside subscription only if the actual line supports the route.** Beside documents call history/analytics and missed-call auto-reply, but the project has no connected integration. Confirm whether Tony’s published number is hosted there or merely forwarded, whether incoming SMS reaches the same inbox, and whether the subscribed features work. Call forwarding alone does not prove SMS routing. Prefer a verified existing inbox plus a manual callback queue; do not assume APIs, automatically enable replies, change numbers or add a provider. [Beside call analytics](https://help.beside.com/en/articles/14601918-call-insights-and-analytics), [missed-call auto-reply](https://help.beside.com/en/articles/15958970-missed-call-auto-reply), [number/forwarding documentation](https://help.beside.com/en/articles/9882761-find-your-beside-number).
- **Separate funnel events.** Keep anonymous campaign/creative UTMs, page views, call taps and draft opens separate from received, qualified, booked and completed records. Never send vehicle symptoms, phone numbers or other customer text into analytics URLs/events. Use a customer-visible campaign reference in the draft, if approved, plus Tony’s “How did you hear about us?” question; manually reconcile source to received inquiries. Record unknown attribution honestly. Do not assume automated offline conversion imports are connected.
- **Keep a minimal deduplicated lead log.** Record received time, source/reference, contact method, local/service fit, reachability, status, follow-up owner/time and confirmed booking. Protect access. Agree a realistic response workflow and unresolved-inquiry check; do not invent a response-time guarantee. For Meta, verify lead access, notifications, privacy notice and manual retrieval before any live spend. No new backend or Bay One activation is needed for a manual pilot, but unreceived SMS drafts remain an acknowledged blind spot.
- **Test and approve the launch path.** Verify mobile copy and the native call/SMS handoff, compare real receiving records with the log, and prove a missed inquiry appears in the follow-up queue. Obtain Tony’s service-area/capacity, timing-process and copy approval, agree spending authority and acquisition ceiling, and verify campaign billing/caps and lead access. Pause the paid test if reception or follow-up fails; review source quality and booked-job economics before increasing spend.
