Repair requests 24/7
Repair requests and appointment requests are taken 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, by form, call or text. Shop repairs at Tony's workshop, mobile repairs from his equipped service van, and pickup are all by appointment; requested times are penciled in until Tony confirms by phone. Calls use AI intake to collect your details; texts go to the shop. Tony reviews your vehicle, symptoms and location before confirming the work, repair location and timing. The temporary workshop and the service van cannot accommodate every job. An immediate reply or visit is not guaranteed.
