How a Workshop Job Works

From Booking to Pickup

  1. Tell the shop what is going on. Send the year, make and model, the symptoms and whether the vehicle starts and drives.
  2. Confirm where the work can be done. Tony checks the repair scope and equipment needs, then confirms a workshop appointment or a mobile visit. Confirm the destination before arranging a tow or drop-off.
  3. Agree on diagnosis first. Tony explains the initial tests and any authorization needed before investigating the cause.
  4. Approve the repair plan. Review the findings, proposed work and expected timing. If the job needs a different setup or further inspection, that is discussed before proceeding.
  5. A final check before you pick it up. The mechanic checks the repair and walks you through what was done.

Tolerance and Timing Checks

An engine is only as good as its clearances and its timing. Parts are measured against the manufacturer’s specifications during assembly, and timing chains and belts are set up and verified before the engine runs. That is the difference between an engine that was put together and one that was built to spec.

Walnut Blasting (Intake Carbon Cleaning)

On direct-injection engines, fuel no longer washes over the backs of the intake valves, so carbon can build up there over time. Heavy deposits can cause rough idle, misfires and lost power. Walnut blasting uses crushed walnut shell media to clean the valves back to metal with the intake off.

Do you rebuild engines and transmissions?

Engine and transmission work is considered case by case. Tony confirms whether the vehicle, repair scope and equipment needs can be accommodated before accepting the job. Diagnosis comes first; any necessary teardown and inspection must be authorized before deciding on a rebuild or replacement.

What is walnut blasting?

Walnut blasting cleans carbon deposits off the intake valves using crushed walnut shell media. It is most often needed on direct-injection engines, where fuel no longer washes over the valves and carbon builds up over time.

Do you work on diesels, hot rods and restorations?

Ask Tony about your vehicle and the work you need. Diesel, hot rod and restoration jobs are reviewed individually because the temporary workshop cannot accommodate every repair or project. The scope, equipment needs and work location are confirmed before booking.
