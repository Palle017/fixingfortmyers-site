Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Perfect Timing Auto Repair LLC collects through fixingfortmyers.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have—including your choice about customer-care text messages.

Overview

Effective date: July 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 21, 2026

Perfect Timing Auto Repair LLC ("Perfect Timing," "we," "us," or "our") operates fixingfortmyers.com. This Privacy Policy applies when you visit the website, request a repair, send a voice note, call or text us, or otherwise communicate with us.

Information we collect

Website repair requests: when online requests are available, the repair form sends your details to the shop’s receiver and confirms receipt after the request is saved. If online requests are unavailable, the form prepares a text or email draft; you choose whether to send it from your own app. GitHub Pages hosts the website, including its typefaces; it may process technical request information such as your IP address.

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, email address (optional), and communication preferences when you provide them.
  • Repair-request information: vehicle year, make and model; requested service; symptoms; messages; appointment details; and related notes.
  • City or ZIP: optional on the short repair form; it helps Tony confirm your service area.
  • Car location: the address you enter or your shop drop-off preference. If you choose Use current location and allow browser access, the request includes your device’s coordinates, estimated accuracy and capture time. Location is included only when you send the request and is used to respond to your repair inquiry. You can type an address instead of sharing device location.
  • Optional map pin: if you choose Use my location and allow browser access, the request may include coordinates, estimated accuracy, capture time and a Google Maps link for your device. This may differ from the vehicle’s location. You can remove this pin before sending. You can submit a repair request without sharing device location.
  • Voice information: audio recordings you choose to submit and information included in those recordings.
  • SMS consent records: whether you selected the optional SMS checkbox, the date and time, the page or form used, and the disclosure version shown to you.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, browser or device details, request logs, and information used to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the site from abuse.

How we use information

  • Respond to repair inquiries and provide estimates, scheduling, appointment, and service updates.
  • Call you about the request you submitted and, only when permitted, send related customer-care text messages.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website and our customer service.
  • Detect spam, fraud, misuse, and technical problems.
  • Maintain records, enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Voice notes and attachments

Website voice notes are optional. Recording starts only when you choose to record and allow microphone access. The recording stays in your browser until you choose to send it; sending uploads the audio and your repair-request details to the shop's receiver. When photo or video attachments are offered, the files you select are uploaded after the repair request is saved.

Text messaging and consent

Entering a phone number or preparing a draft does not, by itself, send a message or give marketing consent. When you send the shop a repair inquiry from your texting app, you are asking us to reply about that inquiry. We do not use this as permission for marketing. If you prefer a call, include a callback number and that preference in your message. Where a connected web form offers a separate SMS checkbox, that choice controls text follow-up for that submission.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for full program terms.

SMS privacy commitment: We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-message consent for third-party marketing. Service providers may process the information needed to deliver and manage the customer-care communications you requested.

When information may be disclosed

We may disclose information to service providers that help us host, secure, support, or operate the website and business; when required by law or legal process; to protect rights, safety, or property; or in connection with a business transfer. Service providers are permitted to use information only for the services they perform for us. Where urgent owner alerts are enabled, a call/text delivery provider processes the inquiry summary to notify the mechanic; that does not authorize automated texts to you.

We may also disclose information when you direct us to do so or otherwise give permission. The SMS opt-in data and consent commitment above applies regardless of these general categories.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for customer service, repair records, consent records, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type and business need.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No website, network, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Website usage counts

The website records page visits and interactions such as starting the repair form, preparing it with an AI assistant, trying to send it, or opening a call or text app. Each event has a random retry identifier, a fixed page category and a broad source label such as search, social, AI assistant or direct. We do not use a persistent browser identifier for these usage counts. These requests do not include names, phone numbers, repair details, full referral URLs, URL query strings or message-link contents. The private owner report shows up to 90 days of activity; older event records are removed as new activity is recorded. Saved repair requests are counted separately from clicks. Opening a call or text app does not prove that a call completed or a message was delivered.

During your browser session, we may remember a referral or campaign source code from a website link and include it with a repair request you submit. We may also remember which repair guide or service page you visited. Editable text and email drafts include only known source codes and known guide or service page names; you can edit or remove those labels before sending. Arbitrary URL values, search terms and other advertising parameters are not copied into these draft labels or website usage requests.

Google Analytics 4: we also use Google Analytics to measure visits to known public pages and actions such as tapping a phone link or receiving a saved repair-request receipt. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes browser, device and technical information to provide these reports. This is separate from the custom usage counts described above. Our analytics events use fixed page and source categories; we do not send your name, phone number, email address, vehicle, repair description, precise location, request reference or attachments to Google Analytics. Raw URL queries, fragments and referral URLs are excluded. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled. A phone-link tap does not prove an answered call, and a saved request does not prove a confirmed appointment or repair.

You can use your browser settings to block or remove analytics cookies, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. These choices do not prevent you from submitting a repair request. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

  • SMS: reply STOP at any time to stop text messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Call-only follow-up: include your callback number and ask us to call when sending your inquiry.
  • Access or correction: contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information, subject to records we must retain.
  • Browser controls: your browser may let you limit storage, permissions, or other site features.

Children, external links, and changes

The website is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The site may link to services we do not operate. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted here with a new update date.
