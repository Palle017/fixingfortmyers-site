What To Watch For

Signs You Should Book

Common Symptoms

  • Warm air or weak airflow from the vents
  • A/C cools while driving but struggles at idle
  • Compressor noise, clicking, or squealing
  • Bad smell when the system turns on
  • You keep needing refrigerant added

Why It Matters

Warm air usually isn’t just low refrigerant. If it’s low, it leaked somewhere, and a recharge alone buys you a few weeks. Weak airflow is a different problem again. Tell us when it cools and when it doesn’t, and we start with the right test.

The Process

How The Work Gets Done

From Symptom to Fix

  1. Review the symptoms, previous A/C work, and when the cooling changes.
  2. Check vent temperature, airflow, and system operation.
  3. Check refrigerant behavior and inspect for leaks where indicated.
  4. Test the compressor, controls, and related components as needed, then explain the findings and repair options.
  5. Complete the approved repair and recheck A/C performance.

What You Can Expect

  • An explanation of whether the issue affects cooling, airflow, or both
  • Leak and component checks guided by the symptoms
  • A repair recommendation before work goes ahead
  • Direct communication with our team about parts and scheduling

Before Your Appointment

Tell Us When It Stops Cooling

Does it blow warm all the time, only at idle, or after driving for a while? Is the airflow strong? Mention noises, odors, recent refrigerant additions, and any previous A/C repairs. Share the vehicle year, make, and model when you call.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does warm air mean my A/C only needs a recharge?

Not always. If it’s low on refrigerant, there’s a leak, so we find it before we charge it. Warm air can also be the compressor or an electrical fault.

Can you check A/C that works while driving but gets warm at idle?

Yes, that’s a common one in Florida traffic. Tell us if the airflow drops too. We test it hot and idling, the way it fails for you.

What should I send when arranging A/C service?

Send the year, make, model, cooling symptoms, and any recent A/C work. Call Tony at (239) 397-2048 to coordinate drop-off, pickup or a mobile visit. Repair timing depends on the diagnosis, parts, and current availability.
