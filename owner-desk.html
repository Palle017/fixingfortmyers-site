Fixing Fort Myers Owner Desk

A private dashboard for the business owner and approved team members of Perfect Timing Auto Repair. This public page explains the app; it does not expose the dashboard or its records.

What the app does

Owner Desk brings existing repair inquiries, owner notes and business visibility reports together for internal follow-up and performance monitoring. Access requires sign-in with an account approved by the business owner. It is not a public customer account service.

Open the private Owner Desk — authorized sign-in required.

Google connections

The existing Google Search Console connection reports this website’s search clicks, impressions, queries, pages and sampled indexing information. Business Profile reporting is designed to show Search and Maps profile views, call-button clicks, website clicks, direction requests and available monthly search terms.

Business Profile API approval is pending. Until Google grants usable API access and authenticated retrieval succeeds, Business Profile metrics remain unavailable in the dashboard. Google account consent alone does not establish API approval.

Google requires the broad business.manage permission for Business Profile access. This integration uses it for read-only account discovery and analytics. It does not edit the listing, reviews, users or permissions. The separate Search Console connection uses read-only access.

Privacy and help

The Owner Desk privacy policy explains Google data access, use, storage, sharing, retention and removal requests. The website privacy policy covers repair inquiries and other customer communications.

For app access or Google data questions, email fixingfortmyers@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, authorization codes or access tokens.
