What the app does

Owner Desk brings existing repair inquiries, owner notes and business visibility reports together for internal follow-up and performance monitoring. Access requires sign-in with an account approved by the business owner. It is not a public customer account service.

Open the private Owner Desk — authorized sign-in required.

Google connections

The Google Search Console connection reports this website’s search clicks, impressions, queries, pages and sampled indexing information. Business Profile reporting is connected and shows Search and Maps profile views, call-button clicks, website clicks, direction requests and available monthly search terms from authenticated Google reports.

Google Analytics 4 reporting setup is in progress. When authorized and verified, the separate website analytics connection will show aggregate sessions, page views, engagement and recorded phone-click and successful repair-request events. A saved request is not a confirmed appointment or completed repair. Unavailable sources remain labeled as unavailable.

Google requires the broad business.manage permission for Business Profile access. This integration uses it for read-only account discovery and analytics. It does not edit the listing, reviews, users or permissions. Search Console uses separate read-only access; Google Analytics reporting requests analytics.readonly and does not change the property, website tag or Google Analytics settings.

Privacy and help

The Owner Desk privacy policy explains Google data access, use, storage, sharing, retention and removal requests. The website privacy policy covers repair inquiries and other customer communications.

For app access or Google data questions, email fixingfortmyers@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, authorization codes or access tokens.

Repair requests 24/7
Repair requests and appointment requests are taken 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, by form, call or text. Shop repairs at Tony's workshop, mobile repairs from his equipped service van, and pickup are all by appointment; requested times are penciled in until Tony confirms by phone. Calls use AI intake to collect your details; texts go to the shop. Tony reviews your vehicle, symptoms and location before confirming the work, repair location and timing. The temporary workshop and the service van cannot accommodate every job. An immediate reply or visit is not guaranteed. We receive a lot of spam. Name, callback number, repair details and a good callback time help us prepare and prioritize callbacks. Vehicle details help, but send what you know; partial information is welcome.
