What is your vehicle doing?

01 · No-start help

Car won’t start: clicking, cranking, or nothing?

The sound you hear when trying to start the car helps describe the problem. It does not identify a failed part on its own.

Read the no-start guide →

02 · A/C help

A/C gets warm at idle but cools while driving?

“Cold on the road, warm at a light” is a useful symptom to report. The change can help narrow the checks, but it is not a diagnosis.

Read the A/C guide →

03 · Battery & electrical help

Battery keeps dying? The timing is a clue.

A dead battery describes the result. It does not tell you whether the cause is the battery, charging, a connection, or power being used while parked.

Read the battery guide →
Need help now? If the vehicle is in immediate danger, seek emergency or roadside assistance. These guides do not assess whether your vehicle is safe to drive or confirm a dispatch.
