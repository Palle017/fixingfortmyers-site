Before you book

Mobile and shop repair questions

Do I bring my vehicle to your shop?

Mobile repairs are available for suitable jobs, and workshop appointments are arranged at our temporary location. Neither setting can accommodate every repair. Tell us the vehicle, problem, location and whether it drives safely. Tony confirms the scope, equipment needs, location and timing before a mobile visit or drop-off.

What kind of work does the shop handle?

The shop handles engine rebuilds and repairs, transmissions, diesel work and walnut blasting, along with oil changes, no-starts, brakes, A/C and electrical problems. We discuss the findings and repair options before work starts.

What should I send when requesting a repair?

Send your vehicle year, make and model, the symptoms, location and whether it can be driven safely. Especially after hours, describe the situation as best you can. Call or text (239) 397-2048, or use the repair request form. The shop will call back to clarify and plan the next steps. Response timing and appointments depend on availability.
