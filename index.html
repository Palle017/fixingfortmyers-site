Before you book

Shop repair questions

Do I bring my vehicle to your shop?

Yes — by appointment to the shop. Bring the vehicle in once your appointment is confirmed. If it won’t start, ask about pickup; if it runs but isn’t safe to drive, have it towed in. Call or text the shop at (239) 397-2048.

What kind of work does the shop handle?

The shop handles engine rebuilds and repairs, transmissions, diesel work and walnut blasting, along with oil changes, no-starts, brakes, A/C and electrical problems. We discuss the findings and repair options before work starts.

What should I send when booking shop service?

Send your vehicle year, make and model, the symptoms and whether it drives, so we know if it needs a tow. Call or text (239) 397-2048, or use the repair request form. Your appointment is confirmed after the shop replies.
