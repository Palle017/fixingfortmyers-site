Before you book

Shop repair questions

Do I bring my vehicle to your shop?

Yes. Perfect Timing is a fully equipped mobile repair shop serving Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Lehigh Acres, North Fort Myers and nearby Southwest Florida. Call Tony at (239) 397-2048 to coordinate drop-off or pick-up.

What kind of work does the shop handle?

Tony's workshop handles diagnostics, battery, brake, A/C and electrical jobs, plus engine assembly, tolerance and timing checks, transmission work and walnut blasting. What your vehicle needs depends on the fault and parts, which we discuss before booking.

What should I send when booking shop service?

Send your vehicle year, make and model, the symptoms and whether it starts or drives, so we know if it needs a tow. Call or text (239) 397-2048, or use the repair request form. Your appointment is confirmed after our team replies.
