Google connections and private reports

Owner Desk privacy policy

This policy explains the Google connections in Fixing Fort Myers Owner Desk, the private business dashboard for Perfect Timing Auto Repair.

Scope

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Owner Desk is available only to the business owner and approved team members. This policy covers its Google authorization and reporting features. The separate website privacy policy covers repair requests, voice notes, chat, text messages and website usage counts.

Business Profile API approval is pending. The app does not present Business Profile metrics as live until authenticated collection succeeds.

Google data we access

  • Search Console: website search clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position, queries, pages, dates, device and country breakdowns, and a fixed sample of URL indexing results.
  • Business Profile: authorized account and location identifiers, profile name and website, Search and Maps visibility counts, call-button clicks, website clicks, direction requests and available monthly keyword impressions, when Google API access is approved.
  • Authorization: the credentials needed to refresh the Google access granted by the account owner, plus connection status and collection timestamps.

Google handles sign-in and consent; Owner Desk does not collect the Google account password. The Business Profile connection requests business.manage because Google requires it, but the integration performs read-only discovery and analytics. It does not change business information, reviews, listing users or permissions. The Search Console connection uses its separate read-only permission. These connections do not request Gmail mailbox access.

How we use and share Google data

We use Google data to provide private visibility reports, compare reporting periods, identify website improvement opportunities and monitor connection health. Google metrics remain separate from customer inquiries; a profile interaction is not treated as an answered call, booking or revenue.

Reports are available to the business owner and explicitly approved users. Service providers that host, secure or operate the private app process the data needed for those functions. Google receives requests needed to authorize access and retrieve reports. Google report data is not published on these public information pages, sold or used for advertising. The reporting integration does not send Google data to the website’s AI chat or use it to train AI models.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Search Console and URL Inspection snapshots are stored in the private application’s database. Business Profile performance and keyword reports are processed in memory for the current request and are not stored in that database. Operational connection timestamps and error codes are retained. Authorization credentials are kept separately from public website files and dashboard reports, in protected local credential storage or private server secrets used by the connection. Access controls restrict the private dashboard to approved accounts.

Connection errors are reported without displaying Google access tokens, refresh tokens or client secrets. No system can guarantee complete security.

Retention

The app caches the latest successful Search Console and URL Inspection report for each configured source and reporting window. A later successful collection replaces that snapshot. Failed collection or missing authorization can leave the last successful snapshot available with its original dates and a stale or disconnected status. Business Profile performance and keyword reports are retrieved afresh for each successful request; an unavailable request does not use a stored Business Profile report.

There is no fixed automatic deletion period for Search Console snapshots, indexing results, operational or discovery records, and protected authorization credentials. These records and older connection configurations can remain until manually removed. The website’s separate 90-day usage-count window does not describe retention of those records.

Revoke access or request removal

The Google account owner can revoke the app’s access in Google Account third-party connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized retrieval; it does not automatically erase cached reports or remove the original records held by Google.

To request access to, correction of or deletion of stored Google reports and connection credentials, email fixingfortmyers@gmail.com and identify the relevant business account or connection. Requests require manual review and action by the business operator; there is no automatic deletion button. Do not send passwords or tokens. Revocation and a removal request are separate actions.

Questions and updates

Contact fixingfortmyers@gmail.com about Owner Desk privacy or Google data handling. We will update this page if these practices change and show the revised date above.
