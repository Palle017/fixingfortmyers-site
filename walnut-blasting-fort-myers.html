Walnut blasting

Give carbon buildup a proper diagnosis

When it is worth checking

Some direct-injection engines develop intake-valve deposits. Rough idle, misfires and reduced power can be reasons to investigate. Those symptoms do not prove the engine needs walnut blasting.

Specialized cleaning, specific purpose

Walnut blasting is a mechanical cleaning service for deposits on suitable engine components. It does not repair worn parts or replace diagnosis. We discuss the engine, findings and access needed before recommending it.

The process

From symptoms to a service decision

What happens next

  1. Review your vehicle, symptoms, codes and previous repairs.
  2. Evaluate the likely cause and whether intake inspection is needed.
  3. Confirm whether cleaning is suitable and explain the proposed work.
  4. Complete the approved work and check operation afterward.

FAQ

Before you request walnut blasting

Is walnut blasting right for every engine?

No. Engine design, access, deposits and the cause of the symptoms determine whether it is appropriate. Send the year, make, model and engine size if known so we can review the request.

Will it fix my misfire?

Only if deposits are causing or contributing to the problem. Ignition, fuel delivery and mechanical faults can produce similar symptoms. Testing comes before a cleaning recommendation.

What is involved in intake-valve cleaning?

On suitable engines, the intake is accessed and walnut-shell media removes deposits with suction to collect the material. The procedure must protect the engine from debris. Access and reassembly requirements vary by vehicle.

What should I send when I request service?

Include the vehicle, mileage, symptoms, any diagnostic codes and what has already been tried. An existing diagnosis or estimate is helpful. The shop confirms the next step and appointment availability.
