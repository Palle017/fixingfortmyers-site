Car won’t start: clicking, cranking, or nothing?

The sound you hear when trying to start the car helps describe the problem. It does not identify a failed part on its own.

General vehicle information · Updated September 24, 2026

Stranded or in danger? If you are exposed to traffic, see smoke or fire, or smell fuel, stop attempts to start the vehicle and seek appropriate emergency or roadside help. Use 911 for an immediate emergency. A website inquiry does not dispatch emergency assistance.

Which of these best matches your car?

Rapid clicks, or one click

Rapid clicking can accompany a weak battery or poor electrical connection. A single click can also involve the starter circuit. Describe the sound and whether the dashboard lights dim; neither symptom alone proves which part needs replacement.

The engine turns over but will not run

“Cranking” means the engine is turning during the starting attempt. A crank-without-start condition can involve fuel delivery, ignition, engine controls or other faults. Tell Tony if it tries to catch, starts briefly and stalls, or only turns over.

No cranking and no sound

Power supply, key recognition and the starting controls are possible areas to check. Note any “key not detected” or security message. Working lights and a radio do not establish that the starting system is healthy.

Useful details you can gather without tools

  • Your vehicle’s year, make and model, as much as you know.
  • Which starting sound best matches it, and any exact dashboard message.
  • Whether it stopped while driving or simply would not start when parked.
  • When it last started normally, and whether a jump-start has already been attempted.
  • Your city or ZIP, whether you are stranded, and whether the vehicle is clear of traffic.

Follow your vehicle’s owner’s manual for its starting procedure and limits. Avoid repeated extended cranking or trying a generic online jump-start procedure on an unfamiliar vehicle.

What happens next?

A jump-start result is useful history, but it does not settle why the vehicle failed to start. Share what happened rather than buying a battery or starter from the sound alone.
