What To Watch For

Signs You Should Book

Common Symptoms

  • Dashboard warning lights are on
  • Vehicle is running rough, noisy, or underpowered
  • Leaks, smells, vibration, or hard starts show up out of nowhere
  • Another shop gave you a vague answer and a big quote
  • You want direct answers before you spend money

Why It Matters

Most cars warn you before they quit. A new noise, a small leak, a light that just came on. It’s cheaper to fix the week it starts than a month later, before a worn part turns into a tow and a bigger bill.
