Meet Tony. Owner & Mechanic.

Tony runs Perfect Timing Auto Repair LLC in Fort Myers. His work covers engine, transmission and diesel repairs, electrical diagnosis, supported module programming and everyday maintenance. He reviews the problem, explains the repair options and checks the approved work before the vehicle is returned.

Service combines a temporary workshop at Bayshore Ranch with mobile repairs from an equipped service van, plus pickup, all by appointment. Not every repair suits the temporary workshop or the van. Tony confirms the vehicle, work, equipment needs and location before booking. Ask about engine work, walnut blasting, welding, classics or paint correction and detailing for your vehicle.
