Perfect Timing Auto Repair

Meet Tony. Owner & Mechanic.

Tony runs Perfect Timing Auto Repair LLC in Fort Myers. His work covers engine, transmission and diesel repairs, electrical diagnosis, supported module programming and everyday maintenance. He reviews the problem, explains the repair options and checks the approved work before the vehicle is returned.

Service combines a temporary workshop at Bayshore Ranch with mobile repairs for suitable jobs. Not every repair can be done at the temporary workshop or at a customer’s location. Tony confirms the vehicle, work, equipment needs and location before booking. Ask about engine work, walnut blasting, welding, classics or paint correction and detailing for your vehicle.
